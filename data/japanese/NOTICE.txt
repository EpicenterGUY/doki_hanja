HANJA LAB — Japanese kanji / vocabulary data notice

Bundled kanji files
===================

1) joyo.tsv
- Jōyō kanji inventory: 2,136 characters.
- Includes school grade, radical, stroke count and old-form information.

2) hyogai.txt + hyogai-meta.json
- The original core is derived from the 2000 Hyōgai Kanji Jitai-hyō (表外漢字字体表) inventory supplied by:
  https://github.com/xiaolanchong/kanji_dashboard
  Source files: data/joyo.txt, data/hyogai.txt
  That repository declares ISC license in package.json.
- The 2000 table predates the 2010 Jōyō revision. Hanja Lab removes current Jōyō characters from the core.
- Parenthesized characters in the original hyogai source are alternate/simplified glyph forms and remain metadata rather than separate base study characters.
- Hanja Lab additionally exposes non-Jōyō characters in the JIS X 0208 Level 1 and Level 2 repertoires as separate expansion groups.
- IMPORTANT: JIS Level 1 / Level 2 are character-encoding classifications. They are not official usage recommendations, school grades, or frequency rankings.
- Current study-base counts:
  · core: 875
  · JIS Level 1 extra: 382
  · JIS Level 2 extra: 2,985
  · total base study characters: 4,242
  · total unique non-Jōyō glyphs including retained variants: 4,261

The official Japanese government reference for the core concept is the Agency for Cultural Affairs (文化庁) 表外漢字字体表.

Additional bundled reading data
===============================
- mext-onkun-2017.json: official Jōyō kanji on/kun readings and school-stage allocation extracted from Japan MEXT's
  "音訓の小・中・高等学校段階別割り振り表（平成29年3月）".

Source repository:
https://github.com/make1s/diglabo-kanji-data

Source file:
- data/mext-onkun-2017.json

That repository states CC BY-SA 4.0 for the dataset and includes the original MEXT source metadata inside the JSON.
Hanja Lab preserves the source metadata and uses this file to distinguish readings listed in the official Jōyō table from additional readings.

Vocabulary expansion
====================
- words.json contains Hanja Lab's Korean learning meanings/examples plus expanded Japanese vocabulary.
- Part of the 2026-10-02 Hyōgai expansion cross-references the Jonathan Waller JLPT vocabulary lists as distributed with JMdict sequence IDs by:
  https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab
  and mirrored/consumed by:
  https://github.com/coolmule0/JLPT-N5-N1-Japanese-Vocabulary-Anki
- The source documentation states CC BY-SA 4.0 for the JLPT list data.
- Hanja Lab stores its own Korean glosses and learning sentences; it does not expose the English Waller definitions in the UI.
- JLPT labels are study-list metadata, not an official JLPT vocabulary specification published by the Japan Foundation/Japan Educational Exchanges and Services.


Staged Hyogai vocabulary (Hanja Lab)
====================================
- As of Hanja Lab 2.43.0, the bundled Hyogai vocabulary contains 742 unique words.
- The word-writing UI groups them into four Hanja Lab study stages: 입문 (entry), 실용 (practical), 고급 (advanced), 심화 (deep).
- These four stages are learning buckets, not an official Japanese government, JIS, or JLPT usage ranking.
- Explicitly curated 2.43.0 additions carry hyogaiStage metadata; older entries are mapped at runtime using available JLPT metadata and the character's Hanja Lab Hyogai group.
