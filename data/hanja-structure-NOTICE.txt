DOKI 漢字 — PDF-style phonetic-component atlas data

Generated file:
- data/hanja-structure.json
- Coverage: every Hanja in DOKI data/hanja.csv (8급 through 특급).

How the generated atlas is used:
- Explicit phonetic/semantic components are preferred when the source provides them.
- Missing phonetic components are conservatively inferred from character structure plus Korean-reading similarity.
- Characters without a defensible phonetic component are kept as independent/root forms instead of being shown as “unclassified”.
- Japanese readings are split into official Jōyō-table readings and additional / Hyōgai readings.

Data sources:

1. Korean grade / Korean reading metadata
   DOKI data/hanja.csv, derived from the Korean Hanja grade dataset already bundled by this project.

2. Character structure and etymology
   Make Me a Hanzi — dictionary.txt
   https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
   The upstream dictionary data is derived from Unihan and CJKlib and is distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later.
   DOKI uses decomposition, radical, etymology type, phonetic component, semantic component, and short structural hints.

3. Structural fallback
   BabelStone CJK IDS data
   https://www.babelstone.co.uk/CJK/IDS.TXT
   Used only as a structural decomposition fallback when an explicit phonetic component is unavailable.
   BabelStone states that the IDS facts and presentation may be freely reused.

4. Japanese readings
   KANJIDIC2, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG)
   https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project
   Licence statement:
   https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
   KANJIDIC2 is distributed under the EDRDG dictionary licence (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 terms described by EDRDG).

5. Official Jōyō reading distinction
   data/japanese/mext-onkun-2017.json
   MEXT 「音訓の小・中・高等学校段階別割り振り表（平成29年3月）」 data bundled in DOKI.
   This is used to distinguish official Jōyō-table readings from additional readings.

Important:
- “확정” means an explicit phonetic component was present in the structural source.
- “추정” means DOKI selected a structural component using decomposition + reading-similarity evidence. It is a study aid, not a claim of a single uncontested historical etymology.
- “독립” means the character is displayed as its own root/independent form because a phonetic component was not defensibly selected.
- Six-Writings labels can vary between reference traditions; DOKI marks known disagreements instead of hiding them.
