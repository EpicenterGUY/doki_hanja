Korean extra-Hanja data notice

Hanja Lab 2.43.1 changed data/korean-extra.json from a broad Unicode-reading
inventory into a deliberately small practical study set.

Current inclusion rules
=======================
1) Exclude every character already represented by the Korean Language Society
   (한국어문회) 8급~특급 dataset, including Unicode compatibility-equivalent forms.
2) Exclude CJK Extension-A / compatibility-only glyphs and simplified/variant
   forms when the regular Korean form is already represented.
3) Require a Korean reading and Korean hun-eum gloss from the Korean name-Hanja
   dataset collected by rutopio/Korean-Name-Hanja-Charset.
4) Require presence in the Supreme Court-sourced Korean personal-name Hanja list.
5) Remove very rare leftovers unless the character also appears in Hanja Lab's
   practical Japanese kanji inventory (Joyo / Hyogai core + JIS1) or in actual
   bundled Japanese vocabulary.
6) Keep the result completely separate from 어문회 exams, grade diagnosis and
   exam-frequency statistics.

Result in 2.43.1
================
- Previous broad inventory: 2,837 characters
- Current practical extra-Hanja set: 136 characters
- Removed: 2,701 characters
- Every retained character has Korean hun-eum data stored in the JSON.

Korean name-Hanja source
========================
- rutopio/Korean-Name-Hanja-Charset
  https://github.com/rutopio/Korean-Name-Hanja-Charset
- data-gov.csv: collected from the Supreme Court of Korea family-registry
  personal-name Hanja search.
- data-naver.csv: Korean reading / hun-eum gloss cross-reference.
- The repository declares an MIT license for the project. Upstream service data
  remains attributable to its respective source.

Unicode / variant reference
===========================
- Unicode Han Database (Unihan): https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr38/
- dahlia/unihan-json 12.1.0 structured Unihan data is used only for
  normalization/variant checks.
