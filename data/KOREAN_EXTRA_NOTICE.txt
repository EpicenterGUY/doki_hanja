Korean extra-Hanja data notice

Hanja Lab 2.43.6 expands the Korean extra-Hanja collection from a narrow
136-character practical subset to a dictionary-verified 1,075-character study set.

Inclusion rules
===============
1) Exclude characters already assigned by the Korean Language Society
   (한국어문회) 8급~특급 dataset.
2) Require a Korean hun-eum reading/gloss cross-reference.
3) Require the character to occur in at least one actual Hanja-form headword
   in the National Institute of Korean Language dictionary data:
   - Standard Korean Language Dictionary (표준국어대사전), or
   - Open Korean Dictionary (우리말샘), general vocabulary partition.
4) Store up to 10 verified local headword/Hanja pairs per character so the
   atlas can show real usage even when the optional online preview fails.
5) Keep all extra-Hanja data completely separate from 어문회 exams, grade
   diagnosis, and exam-frequency statistics.

Result in 2.43.6
================
- Broad historical candidate inventory: 2,837 characters
- Previous narrow practical set: 136 characters
- Current dictionary-verified set: 1,075 characters
- Added versus previous set: 939 characters
- Every retained character has Korean hun-eum data and at least one
  dictionary Hanja-form usage.
- 饕/餮 are included with entries such as 饕餮(도철), 饕餮文(도철문),
  and 饕餮무늬(도철무늬).

Korean hun-eum source
=====================
- rutopio/Korean-Name-Hanja-Charset
  https://github.com/rutopio/Korean-Name-Hanja-Charset
- data-naver.json is used as the hun-eum cross-reference.
- The project repository declares the MIT license for its code/data package;
  upstream service data remains attributable to its respective source.

Dictionary usage source
=======================
The local headword/Hanja mappings are derived from normalized snapshots in:
- dahlia/gukhanmun
  https://github.com/dahlia/gukhanmun
- crates/gukhanmun-stdict/data/stdict.tsv
- crates/gukhanmun-opendict/data/general.tsv

Those snapshots are derived from National Institute of Korean Language data.
According to dahlia/gukhanmun NOTICE.md:
- Standard Korean Language Dictionary snapshot: 2026-06-06
- Open Korean Dictionary snapshot: 2026-06-03
- Data license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Korea
  (CC BY-SA 2.0 KR)

The stored Hanja Lab usage records contain normalized headword/Hanja-form
pairs and source labels. They do not invent dictionary definitions.

Grade source
============
- rycont/hanja-grade-dataset
  https://github.com/rycont/hanja-grade-dataset
- Used only to exclude 한국어문회-assigned characters from the extra set.

Important
=========
"급수외" means outside the Korean Language Society grade-assigned set. It does
not mean the character is recommended for everyday writing. Inclusion means
that a Korean dictionary Hanja-form usage and Korean hun-eum could both be
verified.
